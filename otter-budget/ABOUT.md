# Otter Budget: Money Planner

**Small steps. Brighter tomorrows.**

Otter Budget is a personal budgeting app designed to make everyday money management easier. Organize your spending, follow your savings goals, and see your financial activity in one place.

## Plan your money

- **Track income and expenses.** Record transactions and review your recent activity.
- **Set category budgets.** Create monthly spending limits and see how much remains.
- **Plan recurring bills.** Include regular obligations in your financial plan.
- **Build savings goals.** Set targets, track progress, and record contributions.
- **Review financial statistics.** Explore income, expenses, and balances over daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly periods.
- **Personalize the experience.** Choose from the App's color themes and available language settings.

## Designed for everyday use

Otter Budget uses local storage for your budgeting information. The App does not currently connect to bank accounts or automatically import bank transactions. Financial information must be recorded or adjusted by the user.

Access to the App requires an active paid subscription. Available plans, trial eligibility (if any), and localized prices are shown by the App Store when you subscribe. Purchases and renewals are handled through your store account.
