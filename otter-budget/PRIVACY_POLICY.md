# Otter Budget — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Otter Budget: Money Planner** (the “App”) handles information. The App is provided by the developer identified as the seller on its App Store listing (“we,” “us,” “our”).

## 1. Information stored on your device

You may enter your name, preferred currency, payday, planned income, budget limits, recurring bills, transaction amounts, dates and notes, savings targets, contributions, language and appearance settings.

**Your financial records are stored locally on your device** in the App’s private SQLite database. We do not operate a cloud account or server that receives the contents of your income, expenses, transactions, or savings records, and we cannot remotely view or recover them. Device backups managed by Apple or another device provider may include App data depending on your settings and the provider’s policies.

## 2. Subscriptions and purchases

If subscriptions are offered in your version of the App, purchases and billing are handled by the **Apple App Store or Google Play**. We do not receive or store your full payment-card information.

The App integrates **RevenueCat** to manage subscription access. When enabled, RevenueCat may process a pseudonymous App User ID, subscription status and purchase history, store receipts or purchase tokens, device and operating-system details, and related technical information needed to validate purchases and restore access. This information is used to provide subscriptions, prevent fraud and support customers; RevenueCat does not need your budgeting entries for purchase verification.

See [RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/). Apple and Google also process purchase data under their own privacy policies. **Deleting the App does not cancel an active subscription.** You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your store account.

## 3. Why information is used

- To show budgets, spending limits, income, expenses, transaction history, financial summaries, and savings goals on your device.
- To retain local App data and your preferences between sessions.
- To verify purchases, restore subscriptions, and manage paid access.
- To respond when you voluntarily contact our support or submit a privacy request.

## 4. Advertising, analytics, and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your locally stored budgeting entries with advertisers or use them for targeted advertising. The current App does not include a separate third-party advertising or product analytics SDK.

Purchase-related and technical information may be shared with the store and RevenueCat to operate subscriptions. We may disclose information when required by law, limited to the information available to us.

## 5. Storage and security

Budgeting information is kept in local App storage, subject to your device’s security and any backups you enable. We apply reasonable precautions, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. The App does not currently connect to your bank or request online banking credentials.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Local financial entries remain on your device until you edit or delete them through available App features or uninstall the App. Uninstalling generally removes the local database, although copies may remain in device backups.

Apple, Google and RevenueCat may retain transaction-related information as necessary for verification, refunds, accounting, fraud prevention, or legal requirements. Contact us below to request access to or deletion of information under our control. We may need a purchase identifier to find related subscription records.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You may edit information available in the App, manage device backups, stop using the App, and request access, correction or deletion of information we control. Other privacy rights may apply depending on your location. Because your financial entries stay on your device, we generally cannot remotely retrieve or erase those entries. Cancel subscriptions separately through Apple or Google.

## 8. Children

The App is a general-purpose budgeting tool and is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children's financial entries on our servers. Contact us if you have concerns about information sent to support.

## 9. International processing and changes

Subscription providers may process technical and purchase information outside your country, under their applicable privacy protections. If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date at this address.
